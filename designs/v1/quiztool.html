RACQUET DYNAMICS & FITTING ADVICE

Essential kinematic insights from our master stringers and certified club physiologists on optimizing arm health and peak court execution.

01 / JOINT HEALTH healing

Which racquets are best for tennis elbow & wrist pain?

Frames with a stiffness rating (Babolat RDC RA) under 63 transmit substantially fewer high-frequency vibrational shocks to the extensor carpi radialis brevis tendon. Pairing an arm-safe frame with natural gut or soft multifilament strings strung at lower tensions (48-52 lbs) prevents over 70% of peak elbow impact shocks.

Recommended Spec RA < 62 · Soft Multi
02 / SWINGWEIGHT fitness_center

How does frame weight affect swing fatigue?

Static weight (grams) and dynamic swingweight (kg·cm²) dictate forearm stress. A heavier static frame (300g+) with a head-light balance (4-7 pts HL) absorbs ball collision better, protecting your arm. Conversely, an excessively light racquet with head-heavy balance forces muscles to work harder upon heavy ball impacts.

Recommended Spec 300-310g · 315-320 Swingweight

What string tension and hybrid setups are recommended with APEX frames?
