# Project Brief: INSPO Rebrand & E-commerce Experience

## 1. Project Overview
INSPO is an elite sports fashion brand pivoting from a general high-performance "running/training" aesthetic to a prestigious, "quiet luxury" tennis brand inspired by the heritage and elegance of Wimbledon. The project involves a complete visual overhaul and the creation of a high-fidelity e-commerce experience.

## 2. Brand Identity & Vision
- **Brand Name:** INSPO
- **Core Value Proposition:** High-performance technical precision meets timeless tennis heritage.
- **Visual Direction:** Minimal, elegant, and sophisticated.
- **Aesthetic Inspiration:** "Wimbledon vibe"—prestigious, clean, editorial, and spacious.
- **Key Motifs:** Grass courts, pristine whites, high-end craftsmanship, and heritage racquet clubs.

## 3. Design Systems & Visual Language
Throughout the iteration process, the brand has explored three distinct visual directions:
1. **Kinetic High-Performance (Dark):** Bold, high-contrast, charcoal and neon lime. (De-prioritized)
2. **INSPO Performance (Minimal Light):** Modern, airy, monochromatic with technical sans-serif typography.
3. **Heritage Court (Elegant Tennis):** High-end editorial, featuring **Playfair Display** (serif) and **Forest Green** accents on a base of tennis whites and creams.

### Design Tokens
- **Typography:** Playfair Display (Headlines), Chivo or similar Sans-Serif (Body).
- **Primary Palette:** Tennis White, Off-White, Charcoal.
- **Accent Palette:** Forest Green (Heritage), Subtle Grey (Technical).
- **Spacing:** Large margins, generous whitespace, gallery-style layouts.

## 4. Required Screens & User Flows
The e-commerce experience consists of four core pillars:

### A. Homepage (Brand Impact)
- **Objective:** Establish the "New Season" narrative and brand positioning.
- **Key Features:** Immersive hero sections, trending collections, and "Master the Game" content blocks.

### B. Product Listing (Collections)
- **Objective:** Effortless discovery of curated tennis gear.
- **Key Features:** Filterable grid (Category, Size, Color), high-resolution product photography, and "Bestseller" badges.

### C. Product Detail Page (Technical Precision)
- **Objective:** Deep-dive into product engineering and craftsmanship.
- **Key Features:** Image galleries, technical specifications (e.g., "Lateral Lockdown", "Endurance Mesh"), size selection, and a high-contrast CTA.

### D. Brand Experience (The Lab / The Club)
- **Objective:** Build brand equity through storytelling.
- **Key Features:** Editorial masonry layouts, athlete quotes, "Science of the Swing" features, and heritage archives.

## 5. Content Strategy
- **Tone of Voice:** Authoritative, refined, and precise.
- **Imagery:** Editorial, "quiet luxury" sports photography. High-fashion approach to athletic subjects.
- **Product Focus:** Pleated sets, heritage polos, performance tennis footwear, and high-end racquets.

## 6. Success Metrics
- **Visual Cohesion:** Seamless transition between technical athletic performance and elite club heritage.
- **User Engagement:** High interaction with editorial "Brand Experience" content.
- **Conversion:** Streamlined paths from lifestyle inspiration to technical product details.
